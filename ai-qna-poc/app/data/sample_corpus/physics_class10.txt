CBSE CLASS X SCIENCE (PHYSICS) - SAMPLE QUESTION PAPER (bundled fallback)

1. State Ohm's law and define the SI unit of resistance.
2. An electric bulb is rated 60 W at 230 V. Calculate the current it draws and
   its resistance.
3. In a series circuit the same current flows through every component; in a
   parallel circuit the potential difference is the same across each branch.
4. Define the power of a lens. State its SI unit (dioptre) and the mirror
   formula relating object distance, image distance and focal length.
5. Describe image formation by a concave mirror for different object positions
   (reflection and refraction of light).
