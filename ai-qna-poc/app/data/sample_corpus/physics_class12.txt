CBSE CLASS XII PHYSICS - SAMPLE QUESTION PAPER (bundled fallback)

SECTION A
1. Define electric resistance and state its SI unit. A wire of resistivity rho,
   length L and cross-sectional area A carries a steady current.
2. Two resistors are connected first in series and then in parallel. Derive the
   equivalent resistance in each case.
3. State Ohm's law. Draw the V-I characteristic for an ohmic conductor.
4. A cell of emf and internal resistance r drives a current through an external
   resistor R. Obtain the expression for terminal potential difference.
5. State Kirchhoff's junction rule and loop rule and apply them to a circuit.
6. Define electric power dissipated in a resistor and express it in three forms.

SECTION B (Electrostatics)
7. State Coulomb's law. Using Gauss's law obtain the electric field due to an
   infinite line charge of linear charge density lambda.
8. Explain why two electric field lines never intersect.
