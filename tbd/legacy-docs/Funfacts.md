## 🐾 Animals

### 1. Did you know an octopus has three hearts?
- **Fact:** Octopus has 3 hearts
- **Explanation:** Two hearts pump blood to the gills, and one pumps blood to the rest of the body. The big heart even stops beating when the octopus swims.
- **Visual:** Image — [Octopus2.jpg](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/11/Octopus2.jpg)

### 2. Did you know cows do not have upper front teeth?
- **Fact:** Cows use a dental pad
- **Explanation:** Cows have a tough pad on top instead of upper front teeth. They press grass against it with the lower teeth to tear food.
- **Visual:** YouTube — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/embed/5sLXadq6Y0Y) · [Thumbnail](https://img.youtube.com/vi/5sLXadq6Y0Y/0.jpg)

### 3. Did you know elephants can hear with their feet?
- **Fact:** Elephants feel sound through the ground
- **Explanation:** Elephants pick up tiny vibrations from the ground using sensitive pads in their feet. This helps them hear other elephants from far away.
- **Visual:** Image — [African Bush Elephant](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/37/African_Bush_Elephant.jpg)

### 4. Did you know a butterfly tastes with its feet?
- **Fact:** Butterflies taste with their feet
- **Explanation:** Butterflies have tiny taste sensors on their feet. When they land on a flower, they instantly know if it has sweet nectar.
- **Visual:** YouTube — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/embed/hQ5e3zSAx9o) · [Thumbnail](https://img.youtube.com/vi/hQ5e3zSAx9o/0.jpg)

## 🧠 Human Body

### 1. Did you know your bones are stronger than steel (by weight)?
- **Fact:** Bones are super strong
- **Explanation:** Pound for pound, human bone is stronger than steel. A piece the size of a matchbox can hold the weight of about 9 bricks.
- **Visual:** Image — [Human skeleton diagram](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/43/Human_skeleton_front_en.svg)

### 2. Did you know your heart beats around 100,000 times a day?
- **Fact:** Heart never takes a break
- **Explanation:** Your heart pumps blood every second of your life. In one day, it beats about 100,000 times to keep you running, jumping, and playing.
- **Visual:** YouTube — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/embed/7XaftdE_h60) · [Thumbnail](https://img.youtube.com/vi/7XaftdE_h60/0.jpg)

### 3. Did you know you have taste buds even on the roof of your mouth?
- **Fact:** Taste is everywhere in your mouth
- **Explanation:** Taste buds are not only on the tongue. They are also on the cheeks and the roof of the mouth, helping you enjoy food.
- **Visual:** Image — [Tongue](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/db/Tongue.agr.jpg)

### 4. Did you know your brain works even while you sleep?
- **Fact:** The brain never sleeps
- **Explanation:** When you sleep, your brain organizes memories and helps your body heal. That is why a good sleep makes you smarter and stronger.
- **Visual:** YouTube — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/embed/rh4WzdMgzgU) · [Thumbnail](https://img.youtube.com/vi/rh4WzdMgzgU/0.jpg)

## 🔬 Science

### 1. Did you know hot water can freeze faster than cold water?
- **Fact:** The Mpemba effect
- **Explanation:** Sometimes hot water freezes quicker than cold water. Scientists call this the Mpemba effect, and it still surprises everyone.
- **Visual:** YouTube — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/embed/Hyd0BSyGiFY) · [Thumbnail](https://img.youtube.com/vi/Hyd0BSyGiFY/0.jpg)

### 2. Did you know lightning is hotter than the surface of the Sun?
- **Fact:** Lightning is super hot
- **Explanation:** A lightning bolt can heat the air around it to 30,000°C. That is about five times hotter than the Sun's surface.
- **Visual:** Image — [Lightning over Oradea](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/87/Lightning_over_Oradea_Romania_2.jpg)

### 3. Did you know sound travels faster in water than in air?
- **Fact:** Underwater sound is super fast
- **Explanation:** Sound waves move about 4 times faster in water than in air. That is how dolphins can hear each other from far away.
- **Visual:** YouTube — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/embed/PNd2ueZHj7Y) · [Thumbnail](https://img.youtube.com/vi/PNd2ueZHj7Y/0.jpg)

### 4. Did you know a magnet has two ends called poles?
- **Fact:** Magnets have North and South poles
- **Explanation:** Every magnet has a North pole and a South pole. Same poles push away, while opposite poles pull together.
- **Visual:** Image — [Magnet](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/57/Magnet0873.png)

## 🌿 Nature

### 1. Did you know trees talk to each other?
- **Fact:** Trees communicate underground
- **Explanation:** Trees share food and warning signals through tiny fungus threads in the soil. Scientists call this the wood wide web.
- **Visual:** YouTube — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/embed/yWOqeyPIVRo) · [Thumbnail](https://img.youtube.com/vi/yWOqeyPIVRo/0.jpg)

### 2. Did you know honey never spoils?
- **Fact:** Honey lasts forever
- **Explanation:** Archaeologists found honey in ancient tombs that was still good to eat after 3,000 years. Honey naturally protects itself from germs.
- **Visual:** Image — [Honey comb](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/95/Honey_comb.jpg)

### 3. Did you know bananas are berries but strawberries are not?
- **Fact:** Berry rules are strange
- **Explanation:** In science, berries grow from a single flower with one ovary. That makes bananas a true berry, while strawberries are not.
- **Visual:** Image — [Banana](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8a/Banana-Single.jpg)

### 4. Did you know rain has a smell?
- **Fact:** That earthy smell is petrichor
- **Explanation:** When rain hits dry soil, it releases a special oil and bacteria smell called petrichor. That is why rain smells so fresh.
- **Visual:** YouTube — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/embed/Wci-1c6e2QY) · [Thumbnail](https://img.youtube.com/vi/Wci-1c6e2QY/0.jpg)

## 🚀 Space

### 1. Did you know one day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus?
- **Fact:** Venus spins very slowly
- **Explanation:** Venus takes 243 Earth days to spin once, but only 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun. So a Venus day is longer than a Venus year.
- **Visual:** Image — [Venus](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e5/Venus-real_color.jpg)

### 2. Did you know there are more stars than grains of sand on Earth?
- **Fact:** The universe is huge
- **Explanation:** Scientists believe there are more stars in the universe than all the sand grains on every beach and desert on Earth.
- **Visual:** YouTube — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/embed/oAVjF_7ensg) · [Thumbnail](https://img.youtube.com/vi/oAVjF_7ensg/0.jpg)

### 3. Did you know footprints on the Moon will stay for millions of years?
- **Fact:** No wind on the Moon
- **Explanation:** There is no wind or rain on the Moon to wipe footprints away. Astronaut footprints from 1969 are still there today.
- **Visual:** Image — [Apollo 11 bootprint](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/98/Apollo_11_bootprint.jpg)

### 4. Did you know the Sun is so big that 1.3 million Earths can fit inside it?
- **Fact:** The Sun is gigantic
- **Explanation:** Our Sun is a giant ball of hot gas. If it were hollow, more than a million Earths could fit inside it.
- **Visual:** YouTube — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/embed/lj8FUHrTW2k) · [Thumbnail](https://img.youtube.com/vi/lj8FUHrTW2k/0.jpg)

## 🎈 Fun Everyday

### 1. Did you know it is impossible to hum while holding your nose?
- **Fact:** Humming needs your nose
- **Explanation:** Humming pushes air out through your nose. If you pinch your nose, the air gets stuck and the humming stops.
- **Visual:** YouTube — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/embed/F5KJVrUq0Cw) · [Thumbnail](https://img.youtube.com/vi/F5KJVrUq0Cw/0.jpg)

### 2. Did you know your ears and nose never stop growing?
- **Fact:** Ears and nose keep growing
- **Explanation:** Most body parts stop growing, but the cartilage in your ears and nose keeps getting a tiny bit bigger every year.
- **Visual:** Image — [Human ear](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0a/Human_ear.jpg)

### 3. Did you know a pencil can draw a line 56 km long?
- **Fact:** One pencil, one super long line
- **Explanation:** A normal pencil has so much graphite inside that it can draw a single line about 56 kilometres long without breaking.
- **Visual:** Image — [Pencil](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/06/Pencil-1.jpg)

### 4. Did you know you cannot lick your own elbow easily?
- **Fact:** Tongue vs elbow challenge
- **Explanation:** For most people, the arm is too long and the tongue too short. Try it with your friends — almost no one can do it.
- **Visual:** YouTube — [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/embed/k4ATSYa_xs8) · [Thumbnail](https://img.youtube.com/vi/k4ATSYa_xs8/0.jpg)
