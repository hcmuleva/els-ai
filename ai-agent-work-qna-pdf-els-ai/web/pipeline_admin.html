              What does persistence do?
              
When checked, validated questions, answer options, explanations, diagrams, topics, and source references are stored in PostgreSQL and become available in the Question Player. When unchecked, results are preview-only and disappear from the player after this page is closed or refreshed. Rejected questions are never stored.
